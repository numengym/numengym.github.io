                        How it works

                        1. Move with WASD. You start 16 yards away: switch to Battle Stance and Charge (8–25 yards, out of combat only), or walk in and attack. Press Start for a countdown that pulls automatically.
                        2. Stand behind the dummy: from the front (red half circle on the ground) it can parry and block. You need to be within 5 yards and facing it to attack; Whirlwind hits everything within 8 yards.
                        3. The dummy loses health over the fight length. Below 20% you can use Execute.
                        4. Heroic Strike and Cleave replace your next main-hand swing and are off the global cooldown. Queuing one also removes the dual-wield miss penalty from your off-hand.
                        5. Abilities pressed in the last part of the global cooldown fire as soon as it ends (spell queue window).
                        6. Stance dancing is easiest with the macros on the third bar: one key swaps stance and casts.

                        Fury priority (wowsims default)

                        1. Keep Battle Shout up; Bloodrage and Berserker Rage on cooldown.
                        2. Death Wish (and Recklessness) on cooldown.
                        3. Execute in the last 20%.
                        4. Whirlwind, then Bloodthirst.
                        5. Heroic Strike above 40 rage (Cleave with 2+ targets).
                        6. Optional weaving: Hamstring for Windfury/Crusader procs; switch to Battle Stance for Overpower after a dodge or to refresh Rend, then back to Berserker Stance. Rage above your Tactical Mastery cap is lost on each swap.
